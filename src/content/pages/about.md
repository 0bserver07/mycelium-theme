# About This Garden

This digital garden is powered by **Mycelium** - a publishing system built with Astro and designed for Obsidian users.

## The Gardener

Hello! I'm the gardener of this digital space. I use this garden to cultivate ideas, share what I'm learning, and connect thoughts across different domains.

## How This Works

- **Posts** are garden notes that grow over time
- **Projects** showcase things I'm building
- **Docs** contain technical documentation

Feel free to explore and follow the connections between ideas.
