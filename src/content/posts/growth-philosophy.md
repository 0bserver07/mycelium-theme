# The Philosophy of Growth

In a traditional blog, content is either published or it isn't. But in a digital garden, we embrace a more organic metaphor: **growth**.

## The Three Stages

### 🌱 Seedling

A seedling note is a new idea, freshly planted. It might be:

- A rough thought captured quickly
- A quote that resonated
- An incomplete exploration
- A question without an answer

> [!note] Don't fear imperfection
> Seedlings aren't meant to be perfect. They're meant to exist.

### 🌿 Budding

A budding note is developing. It has structure, but needs more nurturing:

- The outline is clear
- Some sections are fleshed out
- Others need research or thought
- It's starting to [[using-wikilinks|connect to other notes]]

### 🌲 Evergreen

An evergreen note is mature and well-maintained:

- Comprehensive coverage
- Regularly updated
- Well-connected to other notes
- A reference piece you return to

## Why Growth Matters

This isn't just metaphor—it's **permission**.

Permission to:
1. Start before you're ready
2. Publish imperfect work
3. Return and improve
4. Let ideas develop naturally

> [!tip] The gardener's mindset
> You don't plant a seed and expect a tree tomorrow. You tend, water, and wait. Your notes are the same.
