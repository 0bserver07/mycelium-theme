# Welcome to Your Digital Garden

This is a **seedling** - a new idea that's just been planted. Over time, as you tend to it and add more thoughts, it will grow into a **budding** note, and eventually become an **evergreen** piece of well-developed thinking.

## What is a Digital Garden?

Unlike a traditional blog with reverse-chronological posts, a digital garden is:

- **Interconnected**: Notes link to each other, forming a web of knowledge
- **Ever-evolving**: Content grows and changes over time
- **Non-linear**: There's no single "right" way to explore

## The Mycelium Metaphor

Mycelium is the underground fungal network that connects trees in a forest. Trees use this network to share nutrients and even communicate. Your notes work the same way - they're connected beneath the surface, supporting each other.
