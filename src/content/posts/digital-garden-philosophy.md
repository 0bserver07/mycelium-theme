# The Digital Garden Philosophy

A digital garden is fundamentally different from a traditional blog. While blogs are chronological, gardens are topological. They grow, evolve, and interconnect.

## Key Principles

> [!tip] Core Insight
> The value of a garden comes from the connections between ideas, not just the ideas themselves.

### 1. Non-linear Organization

Unlike blogs that organize by date, gardens organize by **connections**. Each note links to related ideas through [[Welcome to Mycelium|wikilinks]].

### 2. Growth Over Perfection

Notes don't need to be finished. They start as seedlings and grow into evergreen content over time.

> [!note]
> This very note is marked as "budding" - it's still developing!

### 3. Public Learning

Digital gardens embrace learning in public. Rough ideas are okay. Mistakes are learning opportunities.
