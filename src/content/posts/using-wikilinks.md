# Using Wikilinks

Wikilinks are the backbone of a connected digital garden. They allow you to link notes together effortlessly.

## Basic Wikilinks

To link to another note, simply wrap its name in double brackets:

```markdown
Check out my [[welcome-to-mycelium|welcome post]]!
```

This creates a link to the "Welcome to Mycelium" post.

## Wikilinks with Display Text

You can customize the link text using a pipe:

```markdown
[[digital-garden-philosophy|Learn about digital gardens]]
```

This shows "Learn about digital gardens" but links to the philosophy post.

## Why Wikilinks Matter

In a [[digital-garden-philosophy|digital garden]], connections are everything. Unlike a traditional blog where posts exist in isolation, a garden thrives on interconnection.

> [!tip] Pro Tip
> Don't worry about perfect organization. Just link related ideas and let the structure emerge organically.
